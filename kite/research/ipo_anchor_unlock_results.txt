FROZEN RULES (restated before any results below were computed):
  Regulatory pin (R1, primary source -- SEBI gazette notification, not a news write-up):
    SEBI (ICDR) (Amendment) Regulations, 2022, notified 2022-01-14.
    https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/regulations/jan-2022/securities-and-exchange-board-of-india-issue-of-capital-and-disclosure-requirements-amendment-regulations-2022_55351.html
    https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/jan-2022/1642395606006.pdf
    Schedule XIII Part A clause (10)(j) substituted: "There shall be a lock-in of 90 days on
    fifty per cent of the shares allotted to the anchor investors from the date of allotment,
    and a lock-in of 30 days on the remaining fifty per cent ...".
    Commencement proviso names clause (10) of Part A of Schedule XIII and says it "shall come
    into force from April 1, 2022, FOR ISSUES OPENING ON OR AFTER APRIL 1, 2022".
    => EFFECTIVE DATE PINNED: issues opening on/after 2022-04-01.
  Era split on LISTING date (approximation, listing ~= issue_open + 10-14 calendar days under
    the T+6 timeline then in force): PRE = listing < 2022-04-01; AMBIGUOUS = [2022-04-01, 2022-04-18);
    POST = listing >= 2022-04-18. AMBIGUOUS gets NO +90 event, is included in
    pooled stats (criteria 2/3) and excluded from the per-era cells (criterion 1).
  Listings : first appearance in ANY series in data/bhavcopy_full; >= 60 forward trading days;
             exclusions PANEL_EDGE / RIGHTS_ENTITLEMENT / NON_MAINBOARD_SERIES / FUND_OR_ETF /
             RENAME_TWIN_PRICE / RENAME_TWIN_NAME / CORP_ACTION_SCHEME / NO_ISSUE_PRICE /
             SME_LIQUIDITY / BAD_LISTING_PRICES / INSUFFICIENT_FORWARD (all listed below).
  Unlocks  : listing + 30 and (POST era only) listing + 90 CALENDAR days, rolled to the next trading day.
             KNOWN BIAS: lock-in runs from ALLOTMENT (~2-4 days before listing); the spec
             freezes the listing-date anchor, so the modelled unlock sits a few days late.
  Windows  : info [-5,-1] and [0,+1], abnormal vs EW daily-rebalanced frictionless
             universe (EQ, turnover >= Rs 2cr, adj close >= Rs 20), returns clipped +/-25%. No verdict weight.
  PRIMARY  : IF [0,+1] cumulative abnormal <= -2.0% AND liquidity gate holds at E,
             BUY E+2 OPEN, hold 10 trading days, EXIT E+12 OPEN.
  Costs    : 0.2%/side slippage on the fill price + zerodha_charges.calculate_charges(
             buy_v, sell_v, is_intraday=False)['total'] (never sum(.values()) -- double-counts).
             Fixed notional Rs 100,000/trade (the DP charge is FLAT, so net %
             return depends on trade size -- stated, not tuned).
  Verdict  : ALL THREE must hold --
             1) mean net > 0 in BOTH eras, each with >= 15 trades
                (< 15 => that era is NODATA/underpowered, a recorded OUTCOME,
                 not a pass)
             2) pooled cluster-robust t >= +2.0
             3) win rate >= 55%
  Clusters : PRIMARY = anchor-month (calendar month of that IPO's EARLIEST unlock, applied to
             both of its events) -- satisfies both frozen sentences ("calendar month of the
             unlock" AND "the two unlocks of one IPO share a cluster") without degenerating.
             The union-find alternative was tried and REJECTED: overlapping IPO waves chain
             every month together (G=1). Event-month and by-IPO t printed as diagnostics only.
  Declared test count: 1 (the conditional long leg). Everything else is information only.

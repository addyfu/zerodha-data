FROZEN INTERPRETATION RULE (stated before any results below were computed):
  A regime counts as "better" than another ONLY if it wins on BOTH samples
  (60d CSVs and release-week DB). Where the two samples disagree on direction,
  the verdict is reported as "inconclusive" -- no picking the sample that
  happens to agree with the motivating hypothesis.

HONESTY: all four strategies are known net-losers in this harness (140+ prior
backtests, nothing has beaten buy-and-hold). The question here is RELATIVE --
which exit rule loses least / gains most -- not whether any of them are profitable.

COST FIX: this script uses calculate_charges(...)['total'], NOT
sum(calculate_charges(...).values()) -- the latter (used by bb_v2_screen.py via
intraday_probe.trade_cost) double-counts because the returned dict already
contains a 'total' key equal to the sum of its other six keys.

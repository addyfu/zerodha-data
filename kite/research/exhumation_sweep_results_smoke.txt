--- FROZEN DATA RULES ---
  PANEL     : data/bhavcopy_full/ -- 3,204 NSE stocks, 2019-10-01 .. 2026-07, series EQ.
  CORP ACTS : back-adjusted via data/corp_actions_adjustments.csv; HALT if any NaN-factor
              action touches a date the study uses. delivery_factor_study.py convention.
  CLIP GUARD: adjusted daily returns clipped +/-25%; HALT if that fires on more than
              0.1% of eligible stock-days (brief rule R9, not a tunable).
  ELIGIBLE  : series EQ, turnover >= Rs 2 crore, adjusted close >= Rs 20 -- at SIGNAL time.
  ZOO       : the SAME 67-strategy clean set as consensus_probe / zoo_silence
              (78 registered - 9 leak-suspects - 2 erroring). Asserted byte-identical.

--- FROZEN EXECUTION MODEL ---
  SIGNALS   : each strategy's own vectorised generate_signals() per symbol; LONG signals only
              (multi-day shorts untradeable), counted only on ELIGIBLE stock-days.
  ENTRY     : next trading day OPEN.
  EXIT      : the strategy's own SL/TP where defined, gap-aware (fill at the WORSE of stop and
              open when the bar gaps through; SL wins same-bar ties), else/until the universal
              10-trading-day time stop -- whichever comes first. ONE convention for the whole zoo.
  OVERLAP   : one open trade per (strategy, symbol); re-signals ignored while a position is open.
  COSTS     : Rs 20,000 delivery position; zerodha_charges.calculate_charges(...)["total"]
              -- the KEY, never sum(.values()) -- plus 0.2%/side slippage.
  BENCHMARK : per-trade ABNORMAL return = net trade return MINUS the equal-weight
              eligible-universe return compounded over the identical holding window
              (frictionless, daily rebalanced; membership lagged one day -- see (2b)).

--- FROZEN SPLIT AND VERDICT (declared test count: 67) ---
  TRAIN      2019-10-01 .. 2023-12-31  -- reported, informational.
  VALIDATION 2024-01-01 .. 2026-07-31  -- the verdict era.
  A strategy is a SURVIVOR only if ALL FOUR hold:
    1. >= 100 validation trades          (else NODATA: underpowered, recorded, NOT a pass)
    2. validation mean net ABNORMAL return > 0
    3. cluster-robust t >= +3.2, clustered by ISO week of the ENTRY date -- the
       Bonferroni bar for 67 simultaneous tests at alpha=0.05 (0.05/67 -> one-sided
       z ~ 3.2). This is the resurrection-lottery guard: 67 dead strategies WILL
       throw ~3 nominal t>=2 passes by chance; the bar is set so chance survivors are
       expected ~0.05 across the WHOLE sweep.
    4. train-era mean net abnormal return also > 0 (no sign flip).
  No per-strategy parameter variation, no timeframe variation, no post-hoc subgroups.
  "Worked on smallcaps only" = FAIL, recorded at most as an unregistered observation.
  Survivors (if any): incubator-candidacy DISCUSSION with a fresh phase-2 spec. Never money.
  Non-survivors: the graveyard verdict becomes FINAL on the widest data available.

--- CAVEATS (stated before results, per spec) ---
  - The universal 10-day time-stop backstop means strategies whose native exits differ are
    tested in a HARMONIZED form; a survivor must be re-validated under its own exact exit
    before candidacy. A -1 signal does NOT close a position here (spec lists SL/TP/time only).
  - Smallcap slippage of 0.2%/side remains an INFERENCE, not a measurement.
  - A strategy whose generate_signals() raises on wide-universe data quirks is skipped for
    that symbol and COUNTED; a strategy that errors on more than 50% of symbols is
    reported as NODATA (skipped), never as a pass or a fail.

--- IMPLEMENTATION CHOICES THE SPEC DOES NOT FULLY PIN DOWN (flagged, not buried) ---
  (1) TIME STOP ARITHMETIC: "10-trading-day time stop" is read as at most 10 bars
      INCLUDING the entry bar -> entry OPEN[p+1], time-stop exit CLOSE[p+10]. The "10 bars
      AFTER entry" reading (CLOSE[p+11]) is one bar longer and is NOT also computed --
      computing both would double the declared test count.
  (2) BENCHMARK WINDOW: the EW leg is compounded over market days [entry_date .. exit_date]
      inclusive. The EW series is close-to-close, so its first day starts at close(e-1)
      while the trade starts at open(e): the benchmark carries one extra overnight gap the
      trade does not. That is the CONSERVATIVE side (overnight drift is positive on average,
      so abnormal returns are if anything understated). The anti-conservative alignment was
      available and was not taken.
  (2b) BENCHMARK MEMBERSHIP IS LAGGED ONE DAY. The spec's same-day eligibility gate is
      correct for SIGNALS (a day-t signal is only actionable at t+1 open) but is not
      implementable as index membership: the Rs 20 floor would admit a stock on the very
      day it jumps over the line and drop it on the day it falls back. MEASURED ON THIS
      PANEL: same-day membership gives mean EW daily +0.2962% (cum +12,337%); membership
      decided by the symbol's PREVIOUS bar gives +0.0902% (cum +296%) -- both averaging
      ~945 names/day, so the gap is pure selection, not coverage. The LAGGED series is
      used. Note the direction: it is the LOWER benchmark, so it makes abnormal returns
      LARGER and this sweep HARDER to fail. The inflated benchmark would have handed the
      stated prior a free win, which a pre-registered audit must not do.
  (3) TRADEABLE vs BENCHMARK UNIVERSE: the BENCHMARK is the eligible universe exactly as
      specified. The TRADEABLE universe additionally requires >= 300 EQ bars of history --
      the universe convention of retest_all / consensus_probe / zoo_silence. It is a
      data-sufficiency rule, not a performance filter, it is mildly survivorship-flavoured,
      and the symbols it removes are counted below and listed in the work dir.
  (4) VOLUME IS CORP-ACTION ADJUSTED (volume / factor), unlike delivery_factor_study which
      never consumed volume. Without it every 1:1 bonus looks like a 2x volume spike to the
      volume-breakout strategies. Turnover (a rupee quantity) is untouched.
  (5) NO artificial warm-up floor is applied (consensus_probe dropped 252 bars; this spec
      does not ask for it). Indicators warm up naturally inside each symbol's own history.

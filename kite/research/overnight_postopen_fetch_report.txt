Mapping (frozen): foreign close of calendar date d -> the NEXT Indian trading day t > d (Indian trading days = the panel's own calendar, from bhavcopy filenames). Signal(t) = 
log(close_d / close_prev_d), the foreign series' OWN previous close (its own holidays).
Alignment gate: >= 95% of Indian trading days 2019-10-01..2026-07-31 must receive each signal, else HALT.

Method (frozen): GAP(s,t)=open(s,t)/close(s,t-1)-1; POST(s,t,k)=close(s,t+k-1)/open(s,t)-1, k in {1,3,10}. Market arm: EW eligible-universe means (LAGGED membership -- exhumation 
lesson), regression POST(t,k)=a+b*SIGNAL(t)+c*GAP(t)+e per (signal,k), cluster-robust by 
ISO week. ADR arm: INFY/WIT/IBN/HDB overnight ADR returns, pooled across names (week-
clustered only, name pooling stated), fade hypothesis pre-registered NEGATIVE b, k in {1,3}.

Declared cells: 3 signals x 3 horizons (market) + 2 (ADR, k in {1,3}) = 11.
Per cell, ALL required for a FINDING: (1) VAL-era cluster-robust |t(b)| >= 2.9 (Bonferroni, 11 cells; ADR arm additionally requires b<0); (2) TRAIN-era b same sign as 
VAL; (3) tradeability: favorable-signal-tercile days (TRAIN-era breakpoints applied to 
VALIDATION), Rs 20k slots, delivery costs + 0.2%/side slippage (market) / 0.05% (ADR) -- 
VAL mean net/trade > 0.

Eras (frozen): TRAIN 2019-10..2023-12, VALIDATION 2024-01..2026-07.
Deferred (both explicitly non-verdict-bearing per spec): GIFT Nifty robustness line, 
sector splits (IT/USDINR, OMC-paints/crude) -- information only, not implemented here.

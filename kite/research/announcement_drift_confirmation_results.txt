METHOD (frozen):
  - Join/E-date/entry/liquidity-gate/CAR conventions reused VERBATIM from event_study.py
    (imported, not reimplemented): E = announcement date advanced to next trading day
    (or the day after if announced post-15:30 IST); forward window starts at the E+1 open,
    one full trading day after E; liquidity gate 60d median turnover>2e7 and close>20 at E.
  - excess_5d = event car_5d MINUS same-era ALL-ANNOUNCEMENT baseline (mean car_5d across
    every joined category event in that era, not just the 3 candidates). excess_20d is the
    same construction, reported as SECONDARY info only, no verdict weight.
  - Eras (by event date E): 2020-01..2021-12 / 2022-01..2023-12 / 2024-01..2026-07.
  - Inference: cluster-robust by ISO calendar week (see cluster_robust_t() docstring in
    this script for the exact formula: pooled mean of weekly cluster means, se = std of
    weekly cluster means / sqrt(n_weeks)).

VERDICT (frozen, per category, ALL must hold to PASS):
  Criterion 1: mean excess_5d <= -0.15% in EVERY one of the three eras.
  Criterion 2: pooled (all-eras) cluster-robust t <= -2.4.
  A category that fails stays failed -- no threshold nudging, no window shopping.

CAVEATS (stated before results, per spec):
  - In-sample robustness gate on data the exploratory map already saw in full. A PASS
    here is NOT out-of-sample confirmation -- the post-deployment forward kill-criterion
    (60 live blocks, realized 5d excess must stay negative or the category self-removes)
    carries the actual out-of-sample weight, identical mechanism to the results-miss gate.

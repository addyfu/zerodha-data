Status: APPROVED & FROZEN (user, 2026-07-28).

HYPOTHESIS: companies choose WHEN to file. Low-attention timing marks news
  management and predicts negative drift BEYOND the filing category. Category-CONTENT
  drift is already dead here (0/3, 2026-07-28 confirmation study); this tests TIMING as
  an orthogonal, within-category-controlled signal.

TIMING BUCKETS (frozen -- exactly three, no additions; assigned from the FILING
timestamp `sort_date` (date+time), never from the event date E):
  B1_AFTER_HOURS   filed 15:30:00-08:59:59 IST (incl. ALL weekend filings, mapped forward)
  B2_FRIDAY_PM     filed Friday 12:00:00-15:29:59 IST
  B3_PRE_HOLIDAY   filed on the last trading day before a non-weekend market closure (panel-derived calendar)

METHOD (frozen):
  - Join/E-date/entry/liquidity-gate/CAR conventions reused VERBATIM from event_study.py
    (imported, not reimplemented): E = announcement date advanced to the next trading day
    (or the day after if filed post-15:30 IST); forward window starts at the E+1 open, one
    full trading day after E; liquidity gate 60d median turnover>2e7 and close>20 at E.
  - excess_5d = event car_5d MINUS the same-era ALL-ANNOUNCEMENT baseline (mean car_5d
    across every joined category event in that era) -- construction identical to the
    2026-07-27 confirmation study. excess_20d same construction, SECONDARY only.
  - Comparison is ALWAYS bucket-vs-control WITHIN the same category and era. Control for
    bucket B = every OTHER filing of the SAME category in the SAME era. NEVER
    bucket-vs-zero (that would re-discover the dead category effect through a proxy).
  - Pooled across categories with >= 100 events in BOTH arms, weighted by
    bucket-arm event count. Category screen is applied ONCE on the full all-era
    population per bucket and then FROZEN across eras (construction note (a)).
  - Inference: cluster-robust by ISO calendar week on the DIFFERENCE construction --
    cell=(week,era,category) needing BOTH arms present; D_w = bucket-count-weighted mean
    of within-cell (mean bucket excess - mean same-category control excess); then
    t = mean(D_w)/(std(D_w,ddof=1)/sqrt(n_weeks)), the confirmation study formula
    verbatim. Full rationale + the rejected alternative: construction note (c) in this
    script's module docstring. FLAGGED FOR REVIEWER ATTENTION.
  - Eras (by event date E): 2020-01..2021-12 / 2022-01..2023-12 / 2024-01..2026-07

VERDICT (frozen, per bucket, ALL must hold to PASS):
  Criterion 1: pooled bucket-minus-control 5d difference <= -0.10% in EVERY era.
  Criterion 2: pooled (all-eras) cluster-corrected t <= -2.4 (Bonferroni, 3 declared buckets).
  Declared test count: 3 (one per bucket). 20d differences are SECONDARY information,
  never verdict-bearing. A bucket that fails stays failed -- no threshold nudging.

CAVEATS (stated before results, per spec):
  - Timestamps may reflect exchange dissemination time, not company decision time. The
    tradeable signal is the public timestamp either way.
  - Some categories legitimately cluster after-hours (board-meeting outcomes). The
    within-category control absorbs level differences; categories with <100 events in
    either arm are excluded from the pool (counts reported below).
  - In-sample exploration on mined announcement data. Any PASS gets the forward
    kill-criterion as its real out-of-sample test -- never an automatic deploy.
  - The same-era all-announcement baseline cancels algebraically inside every
    bucket-minus-control difference (same era, same offset, both arms). It is retained
    because the spec freezes it and it makes per-arm LEVELS comparable to the
    confirmation study; it moves no verdict here.
